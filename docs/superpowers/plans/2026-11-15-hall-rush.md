# 外場 2.0 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the 外場 tense and Japanese all the way through: patience with moods and walk-outs, a 6-group shift, a Japanese exchange at every spot, and a finer look.

**Architecture:** Everything stays in HALL (js/71u_hall.js), driven by its rAF `frame`. Patience drains there. WORK gains a per-job shift size and grade credit. The lines live in events.js `TOWN.WORK.say.waiter`. The dish drawings go in PICS_TOWN (pics.js).

**Tech Stack:** vanilla JS classic scripts, SVG, CDP e2e (tests/e2e/test_rairai.py, PORT 8833), `./scripts/test.sh`.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-hall-rush-design.md

## Global Constraints

- **Each push:**
  - bump sw.js CACHE (`nq-v1.18.0-test.16`, `.17`, `.18`, then `.19` for the review fixes);
  - if events.js changed, bump EVENTS_VERSION and its `events.js?v=` in index.html and sw.js;
  - if pics.js changed, bump `pics.js?v=`.
- **Suite rules:** no level literals (test_pre_n5); ≥10 chat lines per cast member; the CAST_VOICE count is unchanged.
- **Before each push:** iterate on test_rairai.py, then run the full `./scripts/test.sh` until it prints ALL_OK.
- **Tests:** `HALL.pat` scales the patience time and `HALL.pace` the waits. HALLT's start sets `HALL.pat = 1000` (calm) unless a test wants storms.

## Review Focus

1. A storm-out while a question with that customer is open, or while their dish is on the tray or the pass, leaves no stale panel, tray or 「！」.
2. A shift resumed after leaving mid-way counts groups correctly and pays once. Old waiter records with done between 6 and 9 finish on resume.
3. A hidden tab or another scene does not drain patience or fire timers into a later scene.
4. A learner without the words never meets the phone, requests, price or late-complaint lines that need them.
5. Phone width 360×640: the panel, the HUD and the lifted room fit.
