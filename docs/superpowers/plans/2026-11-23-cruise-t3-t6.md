# 郵輪 t3–t6 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Four new cruise trips (t3 電車, t4 月の湯と中華街, t5 千本鳥居, t6 朝市とすし), four new passengers, train travel and outdoor meals for the tour, the harbour's 朝市 and a new みなと寿司.

**Architecture:** The tour engine `js/71k_cruise.js` gains a third way to travel (`go: 'train'`), outdoor meal stops (`eat: true`) and opens a bus stop the group arrives at. Trips, passengers and their lines are data in `events.js` `TOWN.CRUISE`. みなと寿司 is a new indoor place in its own file `js/71x_sushi.js` (like `71v_rairai.js`); the 朝市 is a time-of-day layer of `harborHtml` in the same file.

**Tech Stack:** vanilla JS + inline SVG, `css/app.css`, e2e via `tests/e2e/cdp.py` (headless Chrome), Node checks in `scripts/`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-cruise-t3-t6-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Work is committed locally on `test`; nothing is pushed until the onsen plan's last task (one push, sw.js CACHE `nq-v1.21.0-test.1`).
- `events.js` changed ⇒ EVENTS_VERSION 97 → 98 (once; header, `index.html` `?v=`, `sw.js`); `pics.js` changed ⇒ PICS_VERSION 14 → 15 (once, same three places).
- New js files go in `index.html` `<script>` order and `sw.js` PRECACHE.
- Japanese in `events.js`: every kanji `{漢字|かな}`, above-level words `{漢字|かな|中文}`; `node scripts/check-events.js` passes.
- Trip asks: `pt` = the trip's unit for most, a few review; at least 20 asks a trip; lines spoken only by members already aboard (or townsfolk, `takahashi`, `all`); decoys clearly wrong with a Chinese note why; natural Japanese over textbook phrasing.
- Laughs, cries and stretched shouts are never read by TTS (`{ snd }` / `{ cry }` or a plain rewrite).
- New CSS classes carry a prefix (`su-` sushi, `ic-` 朝市); grep `css/app.css` before adding any class.
- Food: every new food has a picture, a `DISH`, ≥3 look lines and ≥5 say lines.
- No real shop, brand or place names.
- Each task runs its own e2e file(s) and `node scripts/check-events.js`; the full `bash scripts/test.sh` runs at the end of Task 5 (ALL_OK).
- Screenshots of new or changed scenes at 390×844 and 1280×800, day and night, looked at before the commit.

## Review Focus

1. A trip left halfway and reloaded during the train travel or an outdoor meal resumes at the right stop without scoring an ask twice — test in Task 1.
2. A learner who has not opened 稲荷神社/月の湯 by the story reaches them with the tour; the stop is open afterwards and the later opening event still plays — test in Task 1.
3. A passenger tapped during an outdoor meal on a phone screen (390 px) gets an EAT line, and benches never seat two people in one spot — test in Task 1.
4. みなと寿司 entered at night or on its closed hours shows the keeper away and the menu shut, never an empty card — test in Task 4.
5. The 朝市 at 10:00 sharp and at 5:59, and a t6 group at the harbour at 15:00 — stalls exactly when the spec says — test in Task 4.
